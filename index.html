        Clock changes in the next four weeks

          Time zones and clock changes come from your browser's own copy of the IANA time zone database, the one phones and computers set their clocks by. Working days follow each country's weekend as Unicode CLDR records it (Friday and Saturday across much of the Gulf, Thursday and Friday in Afghanistan, Friday in Iran, Sunday in India). A public holiday counts as a day off, both nationwide ones and a state's or province's own (Ontario's Victoria Day, Bavaria's Epiphany), and a weekend day moved to make up for one counts as a working day, from the same table as Who's Off? (2026 and 2027; Italy's and France's local holidays, set by province or city, are not counted, nor a state's the table does not list, and a holiday does not close every workplace). Towns: GeoNames (CC BY 4.0), every place of 15,000+ people. Nothing leaves your browser.
